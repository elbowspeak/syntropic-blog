## Infrastructure Assessment

- **Deployment pace:** Strong and still accelerating in absolute terms, but growth rates are normalizing (75.5% in 2025 shipments to a projected ~42% in 2026). Volume is concentrated in China, and the installed base is mostly short-duration.
- **Bottlenecks:**
  1. **Midstream processing.** Cathode capacity is a third of mining capacity, and refining is China-dominated.
  2. **Manufacturing concentration.** CATL and BYD hold over 55% of the market, and CATL is at 95–97% utilization at key sites.
  3. **Grid build time.** Power systems take 5–10+ years against data centers' 3 years, on an aging grid.
- **Momentum shifts:** LFP has consolidated as the default, and sodium-ion is crossing from lab and validation to assembly. LDES has shown operational proof but not commercial scale. Data centers are moving from passive load to grid stakeholders.
- **Announced vs. built:** The widest gap is the hyperscaler pledge, which has no project or dollar detail. The Portugal plant (late 2026), Changan's solid-state target and the FERC rulemaking outcome are the next tests of announcement versus delivery.
