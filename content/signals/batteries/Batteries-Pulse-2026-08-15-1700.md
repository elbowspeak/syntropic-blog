## Infrastructure Assessment

Deployment is genuinely accelerating — 24 GW planned for 2026 vs. 15 GW actually installed in 2025 — and the binding constraint has visibly shifted from battery cost/chemistry to grid connection process. The FERC six-ISO coordinated action plus the court win on Order 2023 is the most consequential item this week precisely because it targets the actual bottleneck (2,200 GW backlog, 12–18 month DER queue times) rather than adding more announced capacity to a queue that already can't clear. LFP pricing at $55–75/kWh is doing real work on project economics and explains why forecasts keep getting beaten. LDES is quietly crossing from demo to track record (Form Energy's full year of continuous operation is a bigger deal than any single announcement), while data center load is creating a structurally distinct, faster-growing storage submarket with different technical requirements than grid arbitrage. Supply chain signals diverge by chemistry: lithium economics are improving while cobalt/nickel tightens — reinforcing the LFP/sodium-ion cost advantage rather than threatening the sector broadly.
