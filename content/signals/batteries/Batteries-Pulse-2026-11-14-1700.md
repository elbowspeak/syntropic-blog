## Infrastructure Assessment

Grid-scale lithium-ion storage is now in a genuine commissioning boom — record US deployment, LFP chemistry fully consolidated (90% share), and full-year 2026 pace running well ahead of 2025. LDES is transitioning from pilot to first at-scale commercial commitment (Form Energy/Google 30 GWh), though its 40-50% round-trip efficiency means it's being bought for duration, not efficiency. Transmission is the clearest bottleneck: HVDC projects are permit-ready but equipment-lead-time constrained, and PJM's $11.8B approval shows capital is now chasing data-center load rather than waiting for it. Solid-state remains firmly pre-commercial — CATL, Samsung SDI, and BYD targets all cluster around 2027 small-batch production, with mass production pushed to 2028-2030; treat any "mass production" sodium-ion or solid-state claims this year with skepticism until independent deliveries are confirmed. Critical minerals show a bifurcated picture: lithium is oversupplied and falling in price while cobalt is spiking on export-quota-driven supply tightening — two different crises happening simultaneously in the same battery chemistry.
