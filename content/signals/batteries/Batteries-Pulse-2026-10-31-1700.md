## Infrastructure Assessment

Grid-scale storage deployment is in a genuine acceleration phase — both global (53-68% y-o-y growth in 2024) and US-specific (65% acceleration, record-setting quarters) — but growth is lopsided: China drives roughly two-thirds of global volume, and California/Texas drive 60% of US volume. This concentration is the real bottleneck, not technology or capital availability. The $12B transmission congestion cost and the Virginia data-center near-miss are two sides of the same problem: generation and storage are being sited and built faster than transmission can absorb them, and large new loads (AI/data centers) are now stressing grid stability in ways that previously only generation outages caused. Chemistry-side, the gap between sodium-ion/solid-state lab-to-pilot progress (BYD, CATL, QuantumScape) and actual grid deployment remains wide — nearly all of the 2024 deployment volume cited above is still lithium-ion; sodium-ion and solid-state are supply-diversification bets for the next cycle, not this one.

## Predictions

- Given commodity price collapse (lithium -85% from peak) without a corresponding drop in installed capex ($458/kWh, up in 2024), expect balance-of-system, interconnection, and financing costs to become the dominant lever on BESS project economics through 2025-2026 rather than cell costs.
