## Infrastructure Assessment

Grid-scale storage deployment is accelerating faster than headline GW figures suggest — the YoY growth rate (23%) is itself increasing, and the behind-the-meter/utility-scale mix is shifting toward larger utility projects (18 of 20.2 GWh in Q2 2026). The real structural story this week is long-duration storage moving from pilot to backlog: Form Energy's 4x backlog growth in six months, anchored by AI data-center demand (Crusoe, Google/Xcel), indicates that LDES's first real commercial pull is coming from compute infrastructure, not traditional utility peaking needs. Meanwhile VPPs are bifurcating — hyperscaler-backed aggregation (Google/Voltus at 1 GW) is scaling while a legacy player (Sonnen) failed, suggesting capital intensity and corporate backing now matter more than technology maturity for who survives in this segment. The persistent bottleneck remains mid-stream lithium refining: mining is diversified, but the first non-China refineries (Mangrove, Tees Valley) are only now reaching commercial operation at modest scale (tens of thousands of metric tons), nowhere near displacing China's refining dominance.
