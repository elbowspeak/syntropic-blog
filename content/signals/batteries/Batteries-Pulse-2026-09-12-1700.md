## Infrastructure Assessment

Deployment momentum is real and broadening: US BESS just posted a record quarter, Form Energy moved from pilot to firm multi-hundred-MW/multi-year contracts, and China's storage base grew 85% YoY to 144.7 GW. But the bottleneck has visibly shifted upstream and downstream simultaneously — upstream, lithium and cobalt price spikes plus refining-capacity misalignment are squeezing cell economics; downstream, 4+ year interconnection queues mean even fully-financed storage and data center projects are stalling on grid access, not technology or capital. Grain Belt Express construction and DOE's "Speed to Power" initiative both signal this is now the recognized central constraint. Solid-state remains pilot-scale (ProLogium's mass-production claim being the notable exception to watch), while sodium-ion is the chemistry actually shipping at volume this year.
