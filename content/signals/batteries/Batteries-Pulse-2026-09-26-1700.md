## Infrastructure Assessment

Deployment pace is strong and record-setting, but it is concentrated: Texas, California and Arizona hold about 80% of planned 2026 additions. The 2026 US target of 71 GWh is achievable only if Q2's pace holds through the year.

The main bottlenecks in this week's findings:
- **Interconnection:** queues of 5-7 years in major markets are the reason data centers are turning to on-site storage.
- **Transmission:** it is being planned around data center load as much as renewables, and several HVDC lines are still at the "slated" or "expected" stage.
- **Materials:** cobalt and nickel supply gaps persist.
- **Announced vs built:** Form Energy has an 80 GWh backlog against a 150 MWh operating pilot.

Momentum is shifting toward storage as a grid-access tool (data centers) and toward mandated distributed resources (VPPs, state programs).
