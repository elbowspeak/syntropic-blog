## Causal Updates
- **Power → financing → siting:** Credible energy plans now determine which projects get financed. That pushes phasing and relocation, so the binding constraint has moved from capital and construction to grid access.
- **Geopolitics → energy prices → power cost:** Geopolitical constraints are raising energy prices and threatening renewable and battery supply chains (EY). This raises the operating-cost floor for compute in exposed regions.
- **Regulation → grid allocation:** Governments are using regulation and sustainability rules to ration scarce grid capacity. Because EU, China and US paths differ, the same project can face different outcomes by jurisdiction.
- **Falling rental prices and long lead times:** These can coexist. Spot and on-demand markets soften while new capacity stays gated by supply, so buyers who can't wait 12–18 months rent, and those who can lock in ownership.
- **Efficiency and price cuts → inference demand:** Cost per token is falling through FP8 and provider pricing. Whether total inference spend rises as a result is not shown in today's data.
