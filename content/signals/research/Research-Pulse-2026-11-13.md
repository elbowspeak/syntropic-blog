## Weekly Digest

A big week for Active Inference, with two new Friston papers extending the Free Energy Principle beyond neuroscience into markets and a reframing of the brain as a "constructive organ" rather than a passive inference engine. Most interesting cross-disciplinary note: a paper linking JEPA world models' anti-collapse regularizer directly to variational free energy, suggesting deep-learning architecture choices are quietly re-deriving active inference math.
