## Market Position

Causal AI is bifurcating into two distinct commercial tracks moving at different speeds:

1. **Causal ML (prediction/attribution improvement)** — MMM, marketing attribution — is in the *commoditization* phase. Open-source tools (Robyn, Meridian) are compressing vendor pricing power while adoption climbs (14%→27%). This track competes on integration and interpretation services, not algorithms.

2. **Causal inference (mechanism understanding)** — biopharma target discovery, drug synergy modeling — is in the *premium validation* phase. Pharma companies are paying for causal methods specifically because correlation-based discovery has a documented failure cost (failed trials from spurious targets). This track still commands differentiated pricing because mechanistic correctness is the product.

The open-source consolidation (CausalNex's death, DoWhy/EconML tightening) suggests the *infrastructure layer* is maturing and narrowing — a precondition for enterprise standardization, similar to how deep learning consolidated around PyTorch/TensorFlow before enterprise adoption accelerated. Funding remains in the seed/small-round tier; no signal this week of a causal-AI-specific later-stage round or acquisition.
