- **Mid-stage, structurally promising**: Pharma/biotech (causaLens-Syneos, CADS, Aitia) — strong academic grounding and genuine mechanistic value, but the FDA-affiliated finding confirms regulatory adoption is the bottleneck, not the science.
- **Least mature**: General-purpose "causal AI platform" plays (Causa/CausaDB, Geminos) — still pre-seed or early, with no adoption data in this dataset.

The bigger structural shift this dataset shows: causal AI vendors are increasingly wrapping causal models inside **agentic, autonomous systems** (causaLens's self-healing Digital Workers, Traversal's SRE agent) rather than shipping causal inference as a standalone analytics deliverable. The product is becoming "an agent that acts," with the causal model as the reasoning engine underneath, not the customer-facing artifact itself.
