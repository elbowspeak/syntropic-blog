## Market Position

Causal AI is past the pure-research phase and into early-majority enterprise pilots, but commercialization is uneven across sub-fields. **Marketing measurement (MMM + incrementality testing) is the furthest along** — it has a clear vendor-displacement story, an open-source stack mature enough to run in production, and CFO-level demand pull. **Drug discovery is technically promising but regulatory-gated** — productivity gains are real but the FDA hasn't caught up, which caps how fast causal-AI-native biotech can convert R&D efficiency into approved products. **General-purpose causal reasoning platforms (Kausable, causaLens) are still in the "prove it generalizes" stage** — funded, but not yet at the revenue-proof-point that MMM has reached.

The tooling layer is consolidating: CausalNex's death and PyWhy's (DoWhy/EconML) continued dominance means enterprises now have a lower-risk default choice, which typically accelerates adoption by removing "which library do we bet on" as a blocker. Benchmarking infrastructure maturing (CausalBench, CausalRivers, meta-evaluation research) is a leading indicator that procurement-grade evidence is becoming available — this is usually the last gate before broader enterprise budget commitment.
