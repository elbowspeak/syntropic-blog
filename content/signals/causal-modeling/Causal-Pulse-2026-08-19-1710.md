## Market Position

Causal AI is past the pure-research phase and now in **early tooling consolidation with vertical-specific commercial breakout**, not yet broad horizontal enterprise adoption.

- **Consolidation signal**: CausalNex's death and the narrowing of the open-source stack to DoWhy/EconML/CausalPy/gCastle/Tigramite is a maturity marker — the field is settling on a smaller set of maintained tools rather than fragmenting further.
- **Causal ML vs. causal inference split matters here**: The MMM and A/B-testing use cases (CUPED, DiD, HTE, CACE) are largely *causal ML* — using causal-inspired methods to improve measurement and prediction under intervention, deployed by marketing/growth teams without deep causal graph theory. The biopharma and supply-chain use cases (Aitia's REFS engine, causal graph discovery for drug targets) are closer to true *causal inference* — modeling underlying mechanisms to support high-stakes, regulated decisions. The commercial money is currently flowing faster into the causal ML/measurement layer (lower stakes, faster ROI, no regulatory gate) while causal inference proper is bottlenecked on regulatory validation (FDA guidance still pending final form).
- **Adoption curve position**: This reads as early majority for causal ML in marketing/experimentation (open-source tooling displacing paid vendors is a late-stage-of-hype-cycle signal), but still early adopter/regulatory-gated for causal inference in biopharma and high-stakes finance.
