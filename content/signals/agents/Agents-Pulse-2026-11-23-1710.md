## Landscape Assessment

This week's signals show a maturing, bifurcating market rather than a new capability leap. On the framework side, consolidation is accelerating — AutoGen's archival and OpenAI's forced SDK migration both signal that the experimental, research-grade phase of agent frameworks is ending in favor of vendor-maintained, production-hardened stacks. Separately, a governance/security layer is visibly forming around MCP specifically — three-control-point architectures, dedicated gateway vendors, and a real supply-chain incident all point to agents now being treated as a managed attack surface, not just a dev tool. On adoption, the gap between hype stats ("66–73% of orgs use agents") and concrete operational data remains wide; the few hard numbers available (Klarna, Delivery Hero) show real but incremental substitution, concentrated almost entirely in customer service and coding. Capital is flowing disproportionately to the orchestration/governance layer (LangChain, Sierra) rather than to net-new agent capability, suggesting investors increasingly see the infrastructure layer — not frontier agent capability itself — as the more investable, differentiated bet this cycle.
