## Landscape Assessment

The dominant story this week isn't a capability leap — it's infrastructure hardening catching up to a year of unchecked agent proliferation. The MCP stateless-core release and mandatory OAuth 2.1 are the connective-tissue upgrades that make agent-to-tool communication enterprise-grade rather than demo-grade. Simultaneously, the 88%-pilots-fail-to-reach-production figure confirms what the framework consolidation (AutoGen absorption, LangGraph/CrewAI polarization) already implies: the market has enough capable agent technology and not enough governance/operational scaffolding to deploy it safely at scale. OpenAI's Presence launch and Squirro's prebuilt agent catalog both read as attempts to package that scaffolding directly, competing against the DIY LangGraph/CrewAI stack rather than against each other's model quality.
