## Landscape Assessment

The week's signals point to three shifts.

1. **The agent runtime is being absorbed by platforms.** OpenAI is selling the Agents API directly, UiPath ships guardrails as a platform feature, Devin is consolidating Windsurf, and Microsoft is replacing AutoGen with its own framework. Standalone orchestration libraries are most exposed.
2. **The control plane is where the money and M&A are going.** Security, identity, firewalls, and guardrails are drawing the funding and the acquisitions. This follows from the adoption gap: production deployment is limited by governance, not by model capability.
3. **Protocol standardization is accelerating.** MCP's spec revision, A2A v1.0, the Linux Foundation governance, and NIST's Q4 interoperability target are converging. Interoperability is becoming a procurement requirement rather than a differentiator.

The main tension is between embedding and scaling. The available numbers, though contradictory, consistently suggest most enterprises are still experimenting. Vendor-reported adoption figures should be discounted until they are tied to deployments.
