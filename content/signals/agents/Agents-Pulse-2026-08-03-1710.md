## Landscape Assessment

The ecosystem this week shows a market moving from *framework proliferation* to *framework consolidation* while simultaneously moving from *pilot* to *hardened production* — two consolidations happening in parallel. On the tooling side, LangGraph/Claude Agent SDK/Microsoft Agent Framework are becoming the three durable rails, echoing the cloud-provider consolidation pattern of a decade ago. On the deployment side, the orchestrator-worker topology and MCP-based governance/observability are becoming the default architecture and compliance layer, respectively — both converging on centralization and auditability over decentralized flexibility.

The most important undercurrent is that **capability is no longer the bottleneck; operational readiness is**. Benchmark saturation, bimodal ROI outcomes tied to hardening rather than model choice, and the explicit call-out that "context inconsistency" (not framework or topology) drives production failures all point the same direction: the differentiating work in 2026 is systems engineering around agents, not the agents' raw reasoning capability.
