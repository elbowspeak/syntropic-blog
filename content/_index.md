I'm Joshua. I build tools and systems, and I write about prediction, information, and coherence – the patterns complex systems use to emerge and grow.

I co-founded [Signal 37](https://s37.ai) with J. Scott King, sit on the Science Advisory Board at the [Active Inference Institute](https://activeinference.org), and am Fractional CMO at [Xtract](https://xtract360.com); I'm also a consigliere to leaders and volunteer for non-profits. I'm a multi-time founder with three exits, including Kanopy (2022) and WonderTwin (acquired by LocalStack, 2026). Before S37, I built a startup applying first-principles AI to the energy transition.

I write [essays](/posts/) on intelligence, entropy, and the philosophy of complex systems, along with shorter [writing](/writing/) about AI strategy and organizational intelligence.
