Signal 37 (S37) is the practice I co-founded with J. Scott King. We build business-practice infrastructure: the layer above the LLM that gives AI what it's missing to do real work.

That comes in three parts:

- **A model of your business** that the AI has to answer to, built from your own material, with every claim traced to its source.
- **Workflows for your real jobs**, wired into your systems, so the work runs the same way every time and stays under your control.
- **A checking loop** that catches the AI being wrong: before it acts, the system says what it expects; afterward, it compares that with what happened. Small misses correct themselves; big or repeated ones stop and ask a person.

The first engagement is a scoped build with fast, visible value. We build it with you, in your environment, and we're done when your team can run it.
